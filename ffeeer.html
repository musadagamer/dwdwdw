Forests

Forests are large areas covered with trees and undergrowth. They provide homes for many animals and help us breathe clean air!

Rivers

Rivers are flowing bodies of fresh water that usually start in mountains and flow down into the ocean.

Mountains

Mountains are huge rocky landforms that rise high above the surrounding ground, sometimes with snow on top!

Wildlife

Wildlife includes all the animals, plants, and other organisms that grow or live in wild areas without being introduced by humans.
